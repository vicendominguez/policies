Privacy Policy - HomeCare Tracker

Last updated: February 5, 2026

Overview

HomeCare Tracker is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

HomeCare Tracker does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

We do not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Track your usage
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use analytics or tracking tools
  • Require account creation or login

Local Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using Android's secure storage system (Shared Preferences).

Your data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not backed up to any cloud service
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the app
  • Cannot be accessed by us or any third party

Permissions

HomeCare Tracker requests the following permissions:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): To send you reminders about your home maintenance tasks at scheduled times
  • Schedule Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): To ensure notifications arrive at the exact time you need them
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): To wake your device when a notification is due
  • Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): To reschedule notifications after device restart

These permissions are used only for the core functionality of the app and do not involve any data collection.

Internet Access

HomeCare Tracker does not require internet access to function. The app works completely offline.

Third-Party Services

HomeCare Tracker does not use any third-party services, analytics, or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

HomeCare Tracker does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
